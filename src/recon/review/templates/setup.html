1. Your invoices

A spreadsheet of what customers owe you, saved as CSV. It needs four columns: reference, customer, amount and issued. Download an example.

More about this file
  • Amounts are in naira, written however you like: 25000, 25,000.50, ₦25,000.
  • Dates can be 2024-05-17 or 17/05/2024 (day first).
  • Optional columns: customer_id, email, phone, description, and status (open, paid or void).
  • Without a customer_id, two customers with the same name are treated as one. If you give customers dedicated accounts on Paystack, use their Paystack customer code (CUS_…) here, and money into those accounts finds its owner by itself.
  • Upload the same file again whenever it changes. Nothing is doubled; changed invoices are updated.
  • One bad row and nothing goes in. You get every bad row back with its line number.

3. Payments Paystack never saw

Transfers straight into your bank account, and cash. Download your bank statement as CSV and upload it as it is: Reckon reads the date, the amount (or the credit column) and the narration, and skips money going out. For cash, add a line with channel set to cash. Download an example.
